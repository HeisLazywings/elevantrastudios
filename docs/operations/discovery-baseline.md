## 3. Baselines — define before measuring

| Metric | Definition | Baseline period | Numerator / denominator | Source | Confidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Inbound enquiries | Unique inbound buyer requests, deduplicated by agreed rule | [dates] | [count] | [records] | [measured/client estimate] |
| Qualified enquiries | Buyers meeting agreed criteria (e.g. model, budget, intent) | [dates] | qualified / total enquiries | [records] | [confidence] |
| Inspection appointments | Confirmed inspection times (not merely requests) | [dates] | appointments / qualified enquiries | [calendar/log] | [confidence] |
| Attended inspections | Buyers who actually arrived | [dates] | attended / booked appointments | [attendance log] | [confidence] |
| Response time | Median time from first message to substantive reply | [dates] | [duration; sample] | [timestamp records] | [confidence] |

For non-automotive projects, replace metrics with the specific action being improved. Don't describe an **enquiry as a sale**, a booking as an attended visit, or a web analytics event as proof of commercial revenue. If no baseline exists, write **“not measured”**; do not invent a proxy.
