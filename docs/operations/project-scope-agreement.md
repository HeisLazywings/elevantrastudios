# Project scope, proposal & agreement — working template

**Internal template only; create a private copy. Not legal advice.** Have the final form reviewed under applicable Nigerian law before contractual use.

## 1. Parties and purpose

- Studio: [legal name, address, contact]
- Client: [legal name, authorized representative, address]
- Date / reference: [date, project ID]
- Client's problem in their words: [description]
- Intended business outcome: [observable customer action or operational improvement]
- Measures of success: [metrics, definitions, baseline if known — never guarantee revenue]

## 2. Work to be delivered

| Deliverable | Included behavior / acceptance evidence | Dependencies | Out of scope |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Page / feature] | [Expected outcome and test] | [Client content / access] | [Excluded variations] |
| [Integration] | [Test transaction or walkthrough] | [Third-party account] | [Excluded recurring fees] |

Define page count, responsive breakpoints, supported devices, CMS, analytics, hosting/deployment responsibilities and handover files as relevant. Exclude anything not expressly included; list writing, branding, photography, subscriptions, paid ads, SEO rankings and maintenance when outside scope.

## 3. Milestones and sign-offs

| Stage | Client-visible output | Review / decision | Target date |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Discovery | Agreed brief and problem statement | Goals, scope and dependencies confirmed | [date] |
| Strategy | Sitemap and user-journey plan | Structure and priorities approved | [date] |
| Design | Key-page visuals | Design direction approved | [date] |
| Development | Working staging link | Consolidated review and test issues | [date] |
| Launch | Tested build and handover plan | Written launch authority and payment condition | [date] |
| Growth | Handover pack / support options | Support scope agreed separately | [date] |

The actual stages and dates may differ: confirm the table for this specific project. Approval must come from [named decision-maker]. Record approvals in writing.

## 4. Client responsibilities

Client supplies [brand assets, accurate copy, photography, product data, regulatory approvals, domain/hosting access] by [date]. Client reviews consolidated feedback within [number] working days. When dependencies are missing, both parties record the impact and revise the schedule in writing; do not silently treat silence as final approval.

## 5. Fees, deposits and expenses

- Total project fee: ₦[amount]; tax treatment: [confirm]
- Deposit before production: ₦[amount] due [date]
- Milestone 2: ₦[amount] triggered by [defined sign-off]
- Final payment: ₦[amount] due [condition/date]
- Third-party fees paid directly by: [client / studio, itemized]
- Late payment / pause policy: [negotiated written rule]
- Work not included in this agreement requires a separate quote.

Do not assume a fixed deposit percentage is right for every contract. Track **cash received** separately from the signed contract value.

## 6. Feedback, revisions and change control

Included revision rounds: [number]; a round means one consolidated review covering the agreed deliverable. Bug fixes against written acceptance criteria are distinguished from new scope. For every new request record:

| Change | Why requested | Fee / time impact | Client approval |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Change] | [Business reason] | [Revised price / delivery date] | [Name, date, written confirmation] |

No new scope begins until the parties accept the change in writing.

## 7. Ownership, access and support

Define what happens to code, assets, licenses, domain and hosting access at final payment. Prefer client ownership of client-specific operational assets, while explicitly reserving third-party licensed material and pre-existing studio tools as agreed. Provide [handover document, access inventory, training walkthrough, backup status]. Bug-fix period: [dates and scope]. Optional maintenance is a **separate** service with response limits and fees.

## 8. Confidentiality, data and portfolio permission

Define handling and retention of enquiries, accounts, analytics, credentials and client materials. Do not include access secrets in chat or this document. Portfolio permission is **not implied by payment**. Separately record whether the client consents to public naming, screenshots, logo, testimonial and disclosed outcomes. Allow review of the draft case study before publication.

## 9. Delays, termination and dispute resolution

Specify notice requirements, reasonable response periods, work completed on termination, refunds or amounts owed, dispute resolution and governing law with qualified legal input.

## 10. Agreement and kickoff checklist

- [ ] Scope and exclusions accepted
- [ ] Milestones, obligations and decision-maker accepted
- [ ] Payment schedule accepted; deposit received
- [ ] Third-party costs and ownership agreed
- [ ] Portfolio/media permission recorded separately
- [ ] Signatures / verifiable written acceptance retained in private records
