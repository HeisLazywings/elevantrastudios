Private per-client working document. Keep real entries outside the public repository.

**Excluded by design:** [feature deliberately not built and reason]

**Next test:** [what evidence will validate or refute this decision?]

## Weekly client update — four lines

- **Completed:** [specific deliverables]
- **Next:** [milestone and date]
- **Needed from client:** [one decision or asset + due date]
- **Risks/changes:** [impact and agreed corrective action]

Send via the agreed channel; record substantive sign-offs privately.

## Delivery, handover and case study permission

- [ ] Tests on mobile and desktop, accessibility and slow connections completed
- [ ] Forms / WhatsApp / critical integrations tested end-to-end
- [ ] Client access and ownership transferred as agreed
- [ ] Written final approval and payment conditions recorded
- [ ] Maintenance and support boundaries clarified
- [ ] Consent for client name, screenshots, logo, quote, and metric claims obtained separately
- [ ] Published story distinguishes concept, engagement, launched work, verified outcomes and limitations

**Public case-study structure:** Context → Challenge → Discovery → Strategy → Decision rationale → Solution → Evidence & measurement → Outcomes → Limitations → Project status. A chapter may be omitted when nothing credible can be said; never fill it with invented data.
